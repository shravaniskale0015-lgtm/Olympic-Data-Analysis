## Overview

**The Olympics Legacy: 1896-2026** dataset offers a comprehensive glimpse into the rich history and evolution of the Olympic Games spanning over a century. Consisting of two CSV files, this dataset serves as a treasure trove of information for both enthusiasts and researchers alike.

## File Structure

### Primary File - `all_athlete_games`:

The primary file, aptly named "all_athlete_games," serves as the main repository containing essential details about athletes, sports, and medals across various Olympic editions. It provides a detailed register of the Olympics saga, covering:

- **Summer Olympics Data (1896-2024)**: Encompassing ***32*** editions, this dataset captures the essence of the iconic Summer Games held over more than a century.
- **Winter Olympics Data (1924-2026)**: Spanning ***24*** editions, this section delves into the exhilarating realm of the Winter Games, showcasing the evolution of cold-weather sports.

Each entry in the dataset comprises crucial attributes such as:

- `Entry ID`: A unique identifier for each record.
- `Name`: The name of the athlete competing in the respective event.
- `Gender`: The gender of the athlete (Male/Female).
- `Age`: The age of the athlete at the time of participation.
- `Team`: The country or team representing the athlete.
- `NOC`: (National Olympic Committee): The official code representing the country or region.
- `Year`: The year of the Olympic Games.
- `Season`: Whether the event pertains to the Summer or Winter Olympics.
- `City`: The host city of the respective Olympic Games.
- `Sport`: The sport in which the athlete competes.
- `Event`: The specific event or discipline within the sport.
- `Medal`: The outcome of the athlete's performance, indicating Gold, Silver, Bronze, or NaN (No Medal).

### Secondary File - `all_regions`:

Complementing the primary file, the secondary file, "all_regions," establishes the **relationship** between National Olympic Committees (NOCs) and their respective regions or countries. This file aids in contextualizing the participating nations and their affiliations throughout Olympic history.
